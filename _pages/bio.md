Vimarsh is a fifth year PhD student in Computer Science at UIUC, advised by Prof. Billy Moses. His research interests lie at the intersection of compilers, programming languages and automatic differentiation, with a focus on scientific computing and machine learning. His recent work covers AD-aware compiler optimizations in EnzymeMLIR, raising GPU and scientific programs to tensor IRs for accelerator execution, automated rewriting of numerical code, and fast, differentiable astrophysics simulation. He is a graduate researcher with the NSF–Simons AI Institute for the Sky (SkAI) and the MIT CHEFSI PSAAP center.

This semester, he is a TA for CS 598 APE (Advanced Performance Engineering) and still isn't getting much done.
